Closed-Loop Ecosystems for Sustainable Space Habitats

Combining Biology and Engineering for Long-Duration Missions, a literature-based research paper.

Author: Md Nafeesur Rahman, Department of Computer Science and Engineering, East West University Date: 30 August 2026

Summary

Long missions to the Moon and Mars cannot rely on constant resupply from Earth. This paper reviews how engineered and biological life-support technologies could be combined into a closed-loop habitat, drawing on NASA reports, ESA research and ground experiments such as Biosphere 2 and China's Lunar Palace 365.

Engineering on the ISS already recovers about 98% of water in some configurations.
A biological system reached about 73% food regeneration in a 370-day ground test (Lunar Palace 365).
Ecological systems have also proved unstable, as Biosphere 2 showed.
Conclusion: a hybrid system, where biology supports engineering instead of replacing it, is the most realistic option for future habitats.

Keywords: space ecosystem, closed-loop system, life-support system, space agriculture, resource recycling, microorganisms, Mars habitat, sustainable space exploration
